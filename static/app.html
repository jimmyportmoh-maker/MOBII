MOBI HUB

One hub for everyday services

MOBI HUB connects customers with service providers in one convenient place. Customers can request services, compare verified providers and manage bookings, while providers can showcase their services and handle customer requests. Payments are arranged directly with the provider after the work is completed.

MOBI HUB Support

How can we help? Choose a support topic and contact our team.

Help centre

  • Registration: create an account and sign in using your email.
  • Provider verification: upload the required document and submit your business profile; wait for admin approval.
  • Bookings: customers submit a request, select a verified provider, and wait for the provider to accept.
  • Payments: follow the payment instructions shown in your completed booking. A submitted M-Pesa code is not confirmed until checked.
  • Complaints: contact support with your booking ID and a short explanation.
